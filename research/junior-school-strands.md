# Junior school (Grades 7–9) strands

Junior school in Kenya's CBC/CBE runs from Grade 7 to Grade 9 and ends with the KJSEA
assessment. CBC Helper follows the rationalised set of nine learning areas taught in public
junior schools from 2024:

| Area | Topics in the app |
|---|---|
| Mathematics | Numbers (whole numbers, factors, fractions, decimals, integers, squares and cubes, indices, ratio and rates), Algebra (expressions, linear and simultaneous equations, inequalities), Measurement (area, volume, time, money, density, speed), Geometry (angles, polygons, bearings and scale drawing, circles, coordinates, Pythagoras, similarity, trigonometry), Data and probability |
| English | Reading comprehension, cloze, poetry and oral literature, word classes, verbs and tenses, sentence structure, transformations, vocabulary, punctuation and spelling |
| Kiswahili | Ufahamu, kujaza mapengo, fasihi simulizi, ngeli na upatanisho, aina za maneno, nyakati na hali, mnyambuliko wa vitenzi, usemi halisi na wa taarifa, msamiati, uakifishaji |
| Integrated Science | Scientific investigation and laboratory safety, mixtures, acids and bases, classification, cells, health, energy and electricity (G7); atoms, physical and chemical change, transport in living things, body systems, interdependence, light, heat and pressure (G8); the periodic table, water, nutrition, reproduction in plants, ecosystems, motion, work and energy, electrical energy (G9) |
| Pre-Technical Studies | Safety at work, careers, technical drawing, computer studies and online safety, materials, tools and production, business and financial literacy |
| Social Studies | G7: maps, the solar system, weather and climate, early civilisations, resources, trade, the Constitution, citizenship. G8: topographical maps, landforms, climate change, colonialism and independence, agriculture and industry, regional trade, devolution, peace. G9: photographs and statistics, mining and energy, world climatic regions, world wars and international relations, tourism, fishing and forestry, globalisation, international organisations, global citizenship |
| Agriculture and Nutrition | Soil, water and forest conservation, crop and animal production, food and nutrition, food preparation and preservation, farm records and marketing |
| Creative Arts and Sports | Visual arts, crafts and design, music, dance and drama, athletics, ball games, gymnastics and swimming, appreciation and careers |
| CRE | The Bible, creation and the fall, Old Testament leaders and prophets, the ministry of Jesus, Holy Week and Easter, the church, Christian ethics |

Shared topics (for example Pre-Technical Studies tools) are taught every year; written
questions carry the first grade they suit, and easier rows are asked at lower levels in later
grades. Mathematics and Social Studies change topic or focus each year.
