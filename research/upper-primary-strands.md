# Grades 4–6 (upper primary) and KPSEA: curriculum notes

## Learning areas (rationalised KICD designs, 2024)

Eight learning areas in each of Grades 4, 5 and 6: English, Kiswahili, Mathematics, Christian
Religious Education, Science and Technology, Agriculture and Nutrition, Social Studies and
Creative Arts. (Science and Technology absorbed Home Science topics; Agriculture and Nutrition
combines Agriculture with nutrition and hygiene; Creative Arts combines Art and Craft, Music
and Physical and Health Education.)

Confidence: **high** for the list of learning areas; **medium** for strand names; **lower**
for the exact sub-strand split, which follows publisher course books and revision guides. The
KICD PDFs could not be fetched from this environment, so the sub-strands should be checked
against them.

## How the app maps them

* Sub-strand ids are `u{grade}{area}-{topic}` (e.g. `u5m-frac`), with area letters m, e, k,
  s (science), a (agriculture), o (social), c (creative), r (CRE).
* Topics shared across grades share one lesson (`um-frac`) and one question maker that takes
  the grade, so Grade 6 meets bigger numbers and harder forms.
* Social Studies widens each year: our county (Grade 4), Kenya (Grade 5), East Africa
  (Grade 6), with early history in Grade 5 and the road to independence in Grade 6.

## KPSEA (Kenya Primary School Education Assessment), end of Grade 6

From revision guides and school circulars (KNEC's own documents could not be reached; counts
differ slightly between sources):

| Paper | Learning areas | Format |
|---|---|---|
| Mathematics | Mathematics | about 30 multiple-choice items |
| English | English | about 30 items: comprehension and cloze first, then grammar |
| Kiswahili | Kiswahili | about 30 items, same pattern |
| Integrated Science | Science and Technology, Agriculture and Nutrition | 40–50 items |
| Creative Arts and Social Studies | Creative Arts, Social Studies, CRE | about 50 items |

The app's practice papers keep this split and order (reading first in the language papers) at
25 questions each, so a paper fits one sitting at home. Questions are drawn from Grades 4–6,
weighted towards Grade 6, at levels 3–5; Grade 4 topics are asked at level 5.

## Content principles

* All questions are original. JESMA and other commercial papers were used only as a guide to
  format and difficulty.
* Content is light and child-safe; `safety.test.ts` rejects cruel or frightening wording,
  including in wrong answers. History is told gently.
