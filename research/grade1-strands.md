## 2024 rationalisation — what changed for Grade 1

* Lower primary dropped from **9 learning areas to 7** and from 35 to **31 lessons per week**
  (including the Pastoral Programme of Instruction).
* **Hygiene & Nutrition** merged into **Environmental Activities** (4 lessons/week).
* **Creative Activities** = Art, Craft, Music + Physical Education (7 lessons/week).
* Remaining areas: English, Kiswahili (or KSL), Indigenous Language, Mathematical,
  Religious Education (CRE / IRE / HRE).

## Mathematical Activities

| Strand | Sub-strands used in the app |
|---|---|
| 1.0 Numbers | 1.1 Number Concept (sorting, grouping, matching & pairing, ordering) · 1.2 Whole Numbers (counting, reading & writing numbers, place value tens/ones, number patterns, counting in 10s to 100) · 1.3 Addition · 1.4 Subtraction |
| 2.0 Measurement | 2.1 Length · 2.2 Mass · 2.3 Capacity · 2.4 Time (yesterday / today / tomorrow, days of the week, times of day, the hour on a clock) · 2.5 Money (Kenyan coins & notes, simple budget, **balance**) |
| 3.0 Geometry | 3.1 Lines (straight, curved) · 3.2 Shapes (rectangle, triangle, circle, square; making patterns) |

Number ranges used: counting and place value up to 100; addition and subtraction of
single digits, then 2-digit numbers **without regrouping**, with sums and differences
within 99. Schemes of work show "adding single and two-digit numbers" and "counting in
10s from 10 to 100 and 100 to 10" (_verify_ the exact upper limit).

## English Language Activities

Four strands, taught through themes (Greetings, School, Family, Home, Time, Hygiene, …
Conserving resources):

1. **Listening and Speaking** — sound discrimination, letter sounds, polite language, following instructions
2. **Reading** — pre-reading, letter recognition, phonics (initial sounds, blending), sight words, short-text comprehension
3. **Language Use** — naming words, action words, *a / an*, singular / plural, *this / that*, *in / on / under*, opposites
4. **Writing** — pre-writing, letter formation (→ missing letters in the app), spelling, capital letters & full stops

## Difficulty — matching commercial end-term papers (JESMA, etc.)

No Grade 1 JESMA paper is freely published (they're sold per series, e.g. "Jesma 008 Grade 1"),
and copying one would infringe copyright, so **all questions in this app are original**.
The difficulty target is based on the formats Kenyan Grade 1 end-term papers use:

* pictures to count, then **write the number**
* number sequences with gaps that skip by 2, 5 or 10 (`35, 40, __, 50`)
* "number before / after / between", "which is greater", tens and ones
* vertical 2-digit addition and subtraction without regrouping
* short **word problems with Kenyan context** (mangoes, shillings, matatu, shamba)
* money: coins to add, then **balance** after buying
* days of the week ("the day after Tuesday"), the clock to the hour
* English: initial sounds, rhyme, *a/an*, plurals, missing letters, a 3–4 sentence passage with questions

### The ladder (each sub-strand)

| Level | Name | What it feels like |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Seed | Recognise, with pictures. Tiny numbers. |
| 2 | Sprout | Recall with fewer picture clues. |
| 3 | Sapling | Apply: written numbers, two steps of thinking. |
| 4 | Acacia | End-term paper standard. |
| 5 | Baobab | Hardest end-term questions: word problems, missing parts, multi-step. |

Each quiz starts one notch below its level and climbs (`L-1, L-1, L, L, L, L, L, L`), so the
difficulty rises gradually inside a quiz as well as across levels. Each strand ends with a
**Baobab Challenge** that mixes all of its sub-strands at levels 4–5.
