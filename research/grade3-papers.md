# Grade 3: what the reference papers ask

_Analysed October 2026 from three papers supplied by the project owner: JESMA 004 Grade 3
2025 (English, Maths, Creative Arts, Integrated Environmental + CRE/IRE, Kiswahili), a KSC
003 Grade 3 Environmental Activities paper, and the "Developing Reading Power" Grade 3
comprehension book._

The papers were used only to set **question types and difficulty**. Every question in the app
is newly written: different numbers, names, stories and wording.

## English (JESMA 004)

Do/does/did/done; small letters; of/under/in; possessives (hers, ours, mine) and Who/What;
plurals of child, man, mouse, lady; alphabetical order; opposites (narrow, bitter); "Who are
they?" jobs; a comprehension passage with 5 questions. The reading book adds stories with 10
questions mixing details, "which word tells about…", totals and simple inference. All of
these are covered in the Grade 3 English sub-strands.

## Environmental (JESMA 004 + KSC 003)

Bedding materials; bedwetting True/False; the three food groups (energy-giving,
body-building or proteins, protective); strangers; recycled water is treated; umbrellas when
rainy; drought; a balanced diet keeps us healthy; emotions; safe places; road signs; table
manners (eat at the same pace, don't talk with food in your mouth); a clean sleeping area;
underweight; caring for books; washing plates; stagnant water; warm clothes; drought kills
animals; plants need rain; planting trees to care for soil; cleaning latrines; manure;
re-using water; a clean environment; thanksgiving; materials for making beds; mud; swimming
makes water unsafe to drink; oral habits Yes/No.

## Kiswahili (JESMA 004)

Kusikiliza na kuzungumza (scouts, deputy head teacher, uses of trees, flag colours, shapes);
words with the -ch- sound; jaza pengo with tools and jobs (ufagio, kifyekeo, dodoki, dobi,
jaa la taka); rearranging scrambled letters; hili/haya; kukanusha in present, future and past;
kinyume; ufahamu with 5 questions.

## Creative Arts and CRE (JESMA 004)

Painting and drawing tools, the palette, collage, clay for modelling, twisting (rope), pitch
and tempo, string instruments, rhythm by clapping, skipping and hopping, safety, the National
Anthem (prayer, three verses, two languages, missing words), hymns. CRE: good choices
True/False, Moses and the burning bush, Jonah and Nineveh, God's likeness, Naaman and leprosy
(seven times in the Jordan), King Darius and Daniel, Nebuchadnezzar and the three Hebrew men,
prayer, the Garden of Eden, the extended family.

IRE also appears in the paper but is not in the app yet: its content should be written or
reviewed by an IRE teacher.
