# Grade 2: strands and sub-strands used in the app

_Researched October 2026. KICD's own PDFs could not be opened from the build environment
(kicd.ac.ke and the mirror sites fail to load), so this comes from web search results._

| Learning area | Confidence | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| Environmental Activities | **High** | The published Grade 2 design (KICD, rationalised 2024): 1.0 Social Environment (1.1 Our Home, 1.2 Family Needs and Wants, 1.3 Our School, 1.4 Our National Flag, 1.5 Our Rights and Responsibilities, 1.6 Our Market), 2.0 Natural Environment (2.1 Weather, 2.2 Soil, 2.3 Light), 3.0 Resources in Our Environment (3.1 Water, 3.2 Plants, 3.3 Animals). Our Home 16 lessons; Family Needs and Wants, Our School and Our National Flag 8 each; Rights and Responsibilities 6. |
| Mathematical Activities | Medium | Three strands (Numbers, Measurement, Geometry; about 150 lessons). Numbers is the largest strand (about 90 lessons): number concept, whole numbers, addition, subtraction. Multiplication, division and fractions follow the Grade 1 to 3 progression and still need checking. Numbers to 100. |
| English Language Activities | Medium | Four strands as in Grades 1 and 3 (Listening and Speaking, Reading, Language Use, Writing). Grade 2 papers test phonics and spelling, language and comprehension. |
| Kiswahili | Medium | Papers are set out as Kusikiliza na Kuzungumza, Kusoma (kusoma kwa sauti, ufahamu) and Sarufi; sub-strands follow that layout. |
| Creative Activities | Medium | Creating and Exploration (picture making, rhythm, melody) and Performing and Display (singing games, movement including turning) appear in the revised design and schemes. Ball skills and water safety follow Grade 1 to 3 practice. |
| CRE | Medium | Creation (self-awareness, my family, creation of the sky, sun, moon and stars), the Holy Bible as a guide. Jesus' childhood and Christian living follow Grade 1 to 3 practice. |

**Before launch:** a Grade 2 teacher should check the medium-confidence areas against the
KICD PDFs. Renaming or moving a sub-strand only needs `src/curriculum/grade2.ts` and the
matching keys in the Grade 2 question and lesson files.

Sources: the KICD Grade 2 Environmental Activities design (copies on eeducationgroup.com and
doyenpublishers.com), cbcapp.co.ke's analysis of the 2024 rationalisation, Elimu Library's
Grade 2 Mathematics schemes summary, Grade 2 end-term papers on easyelimu.com, and Grade 2
CRE and Creative Arts design summaries on Studocu and Kenyaplex.
